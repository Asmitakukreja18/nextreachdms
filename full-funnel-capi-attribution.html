Frequently Asked Questions About CAPI & Attribution

Why isn't standard Shopify/WooCommerce pixel enough anymore? +
Default browser integrations get severely degraded by Apple's iOS 14.5+ ATT framework, Safari Intelligent Tracking Prevention (ITP) which wipes cookies in 24 hours, and ad blockers used by 40%+ of internet users. Server-side CAPI bypasses the browser completely.
What is Event Match Quality (EMQ) and why does it matter? +
EMQ is Meta's score (from 1 to 10) evaluating how effectively your customer data matches with actual Meta user profiles. Most DIY setups score between 4.0 and 6.0. NextReach DMS engineers setups that reliably hit 8.5 to 9.5, dramatically dropping ad costs.
How long does it take to implement server-side tracking? +
Our data engineering team configures, tests, and validates server-side CAPI, Google Enhanced Conversions, and event deduplication within 72 to 96 hours.
